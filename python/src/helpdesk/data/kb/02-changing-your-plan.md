# Changing your plan
tags: billing, plan, invoice

## Upgrading

An upgrade from Free to Pro, or from Pro to Business, takes effect at once. We charge the difference for the rest of the current billing period straight away.

## Downgrading

Plan changes take effect at the next billing date. Until then you keep the features of your current plan, and you are billed for it. Your projects are kept, but anything over the lower plan's limits becomes read-only.

## Refunds

Refunds are not automatic. An owner can request one within 14 days of a charge, from Billing, then History, by choosing Request a refund beside the charge. We refund to the card that was charged, and it takes 5 to 10 business days to appear.
