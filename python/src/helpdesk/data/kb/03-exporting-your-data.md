# Exporting your data
tags: export, csv, data

## Export to CSV

Settings, then Export, produces a CSV of every project you own. Each project becomes one file, and the files arrive together in one zip archive.

## Large exports

An export with more than 50,000 tasks is prepared in the background. We email you a download link when it is ready, and the link expires after 7 days.

## What an export leaves out

Attachments are not included in a CSV export. Download them from each project's Files tab instead. Comments are included, in a separate column.
