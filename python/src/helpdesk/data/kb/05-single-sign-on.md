# Signing in with single sign-on
tags: sso, saml, login

## Who can use it

Single sign-on (SSO) is part of the Business plan. A workspace owner connects your company's identity provider under Settings, then Security. After that, members sign in with their company account instead of a password.

## Error E-4012: account not linked

Error E-4012 means your company account isn't linked to a member of this workspace. Ask a workspace owner to invite you with the same email address your company account uses. The link is made the first time you sign in after accepting.

## Turning single sign-on off

When an owner turns single sign-on off, members sign in with a password again. Each member sets one through the Forgot password link.
