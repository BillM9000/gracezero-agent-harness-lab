# Inviting teammates
tags: team, invite, seats

## Send an invitation

Owners and admins can invite people from Settings, then Members, by entering their email addresses. Each person gets an email with a link to join the workspace.

## Invitations expire

An invitation expires after 7 days. If someone's has expired, open Settings, then Members, and choose Resend beside their name. Resending cancels the old link.

## Seats and billing

Every member uses a paid seat on Pro and Business, and guests are free. When you add a member partway through a billing period, we charge for the rest of that period only. Removing a member frees the seat at once, but we don't refund the unused part of the period.
