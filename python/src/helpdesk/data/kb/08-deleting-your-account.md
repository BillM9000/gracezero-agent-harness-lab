# Deleting your account
tags: account, delete, close

## Before you delete

Export anything you want to keep first; see Exporting your data. If you own a workspace, transfer ownership or delete the workspace before you delete your account.

## What happens when you delete

Open Settings, then Account, and choose Delete my account. Your account is closed at once, and your data is kept for a 30-day grace period. After 30 days it is permanently erased, and we can't recover it.

## Changing your mind

To undo a deletion, sign in again within the 30-day grace period and choose Restore my account. Everything comes back as it was.
