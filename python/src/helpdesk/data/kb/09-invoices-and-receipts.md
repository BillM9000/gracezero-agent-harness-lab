# Invoices and receipts
tags: billing, invoice, receipt

## Finding your invoices

Every charge has an invoice. The owner finds them under Billing, then History, and can download each one as a PDF.

## Company details and tax numbers

To show your company's name, address or VAT number on invoices, add them under Billing, then Details. They appear on every invoice from the next charge on. We can't change an invoice that has already been issued.

## Failed payments

If a card payment fails, we try again three times over 7 days and email the owner each time. If the last try fails, the workspace moves to the Free plan, and nothing is deleted.
