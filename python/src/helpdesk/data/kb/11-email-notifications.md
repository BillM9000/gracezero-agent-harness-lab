# Email notifications
tags: notifications, email, digest

## The daily digest

By default we send one digest email a day, at 8 in the morning in your time zone, listing what changed in your projects. Change the time, or switch to a weekly digest, under Settings, then Notifications.

## Turning email off

Under Settings, then Notifications, you can turn off each kind of email. Security emails, such as password resets and sign-ins from a new device, can't be turned off.

## Our emails land in spam

Add our sending address to your contacts, or ask your email administrator to allow it. Mark one of our emails as not spam, and later ones usually arrive in your inbox.
