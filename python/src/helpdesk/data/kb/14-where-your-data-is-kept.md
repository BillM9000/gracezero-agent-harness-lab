# Where your data is kept
tags: privacy, region, backup

## Data regions

When you create a workspace, you choose where its data is stored: the United States or the European Union. The region can't be changed later; to move, export your data and import it into a new workspace.

## Backups

We back up every workspace daily and keep each backup for 30 days. Backups are for recovering from our mistakes: we can't restore a single project or task that someone deleted by accident.
